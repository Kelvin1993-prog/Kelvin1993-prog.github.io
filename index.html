About Me

I'm an Analytics Engineer with five years of experience building data warehouses and pipelines that financial services teams depend on.

Most of my career has been in fintech and financial services, where data quality matters and a broken pipeline has real consequences. At JadaSquad I design Snowflake systems that process over 10 million transactions a month. I have brought compute costs down by 28%, keeping pipeline uptime at 99.8%, and cut reporting errors by 45% through automated dbt quality checks.

Before that I spent four years as a Senior Data Analyst at RemotePass and eTranzact International, building the metric layers, dashboards, and reporting infrastructure used at the executive level. I got my start in data at Lagos Business School supporting econometrics research, and I am currently finishing an MSc in Data Science at the University of Salford.
